## 1. SEARCH INTENT
Creator buying gear. Comparison-shopping, need to know what to buy in what order. Write for the buyer.

## 3. TOP RESULT FORMAT
Gear-list-plus-tiers. Category-by-category (mic, interface, headphones, camera, lighting, treatment, software) with budget/mid/pro picks each. 2,200-2,800 words.

## 4. WORD COUNT TARGET
2,200-2,800 words.

## 5. CONTENT GAPS
1. Acoustic treatment as first-class section (buried in one line by every competitor — highest ROI improvement)
2. Self-noise spec explained (dB-A, target ≤15 dB-A for ASMR)
3. Binaural vs stereo vs mono decision (when is 3Dio worth $500 vs Blue Yeti stereo mode at $130?)
4. Gain staging / preamp quality (cheap USB mic preamps add hiss)
5. "Treat the room first" framing — no competitor frames it this way
6. Props/triggers as gear category (brushes, crinkle paper, tapping objects)

## 6. GEAR CATEGORIES
- Microphone (condenser, USB vs XLR decision)
- Audio Interface (Focusrite Scarlett Solo ~$120 — only needed for XLR)
- Closed-back Headphones (Sony MDR-7506, ATH-M50x)
- Camera (phone OK to start, Sony ZV-1 or Canon G7X Mark II as upgrade)
- Lighting (softbox beats ring light for ASMR aesthetics)
- Acoustic Treatment (foam panels, moving blankets, closet recording)
- Mic Accessories (shock mount, boom arm, windscreen)
- Software (Audacity free, GarageBand free Mac, iZotope RX pro)
- Props/Triggers (brushes, textured paper, glass, slime)

## 7. BUDGET TIERS
- Starter ($150-200): Blue Yeti USB ~$120, shock mount + boom arm ~$40, Audacity free, phone camera, moving blanket $20
- Mid ($700-900): RODE NT1 5th Gen ~$250, Focusrite Scarlett Solo ~$120, ATH-M50x ~$149, ring light ~$50, foam panels ~$40
- Pro ($2,500-4,000): 3Dio binaural ~$500, Neumann TLM 102 ~$900, UA Apollo interface ~$500, Sony a6400 ~$1,000, softbox kit ~$150, bass traps ~$300, iZotope RX ~$300

## 8. REDDIT COMMUNITY LANGUAGE
1. "Just use your phone to start — learn what you need before spending"
2. "The room matters more than the mic"
3. "Blue Yeti hiss problem is real — keep gain low, get closer to source"
4. "Don't buy cardioid-only for ASMR — want omni or stereo for 3D feel"
5. "Everyone starts with the Blue Yeti, half upgrade within 6 months"

## 9. MUST-HAVE vs NICE-TO-HAVE
Must-Have Day 1: condenser mic (USB fine), acoustic treatment (closet/blankets), closed-back headphones, free DAW
Nice-to-Have Later: audio interface, dedicated camera, lighting, binaural mic (only if style warrants), iZotope RX, shock mount + boom arm

## 10. BIGGEST BEGINNER MISTAKES
1. Buying dynamic mic (SM7B) instead of condenser — dynamics need loud sources, ASMR is quiet
2. Skipping acoustic treatment and blaming the mic
3. Setting gain too high — raises noise floor, reveals preamp hiss
4. Using open-back headphones — they bleed into the mic
5. Recording with running PC nearby — fan noise on sensitive condenser
6. Buying binaural mic too early — $500 wasted before knowing content style
7. No shock mount/boom arm — desk vibrations become low-frequency rumble
