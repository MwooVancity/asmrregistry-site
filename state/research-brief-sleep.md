## Sleep ASMR — Content Brief for asmrregistry.com

### 1. SEARCH INTENT

Intent splits three ways:

- **Skeptic/newcomer** (40%): "Does this actually work? What is it?" — wants science validation before committing to a video
- **Active user** (45%): "What triggers are best for sleep specifically?" — already knows ASMR, wants curation
- **Chronic sleep problem seeker** (15%): "I have insomnia/anxiety, can ASMR help?"

Key insight: **82% of all ASMR viewers use it specifically to sleep.** Sleep IS the primary use case. The page should treat this as the hub, not a subcategory.

### 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY

**Format that wins: paragraph snippet** for "what is ASMR for sleep" and **ordered list snippet** for "best ASMR triggers for sleep."

SleepFoundation.org dominates the paragraph snippet. To displace it: one sentence defining sleep ASMR + one sentence on mechanism + one sentence on what to expect. Under 60 words, no fluff.

**Table snippet** is NOT currently occupied — a comparison table of trigger type / intensity level / best for (falling asleep vs. staying asleep) would be a gap worth filling.

### 5. SERP CONTENT TYPE

Two formats split the top 10:
- **Long-form guides** (SleepFoundation, MantaSleep, BetterSleep): 1,100–3,500 words, educational, science-backed
- **YouTube videos**: Gibi ASMR, ASMR Darling — 697M+ views on top videos

**What is NOT in top 10:** a true directory/index page. No one ranking a curated, filterable list of sleep ASMR videos organized by trigger type.

### 6. RECOMMENDED SECTIONS

1. **What sleep ASMR is and why it works** — 150-word science primer (dopamine, heart rate drop). Snippet bait.
2. **Who responds to ASMR (and who doesn't)** — sensory sensitivity, misophonia risk, conditioned response timeline
3. **Best ASMR triggers for sleep, ranked** — ordered by effectiveness for sleep specifically. Whispering, tapping, rain, roleplay, binaural sounds.
4. **Sleep ASMR vs. white noise / brown noise** — PAA question + content gap. Table format.
5. **How to use ASMR for sleep (practical setup)** — headphones vs. speakers, screen dimming, volume level
6. **Curated sleep ASMR videos** — filterable by trigger, length, creator
7. **FAQ** — answer the 8 PAA questions; each under 60 words

### 7. CONTENT GAPS

- **No trigger-by-trigger effectiveness comparison** — none rank triggers specifically for sleep onset vs. staying asleep
- **No timeline guidance** — "How long until ASMR starts working?" Newcomers give up after one session
- **No ASMR vs. white noise comparison table**
- **No guidance for people who can't feel tingles** — nobody covers the conditioned response path
- **No curated video index**
- **No audio-only ASMR for sleep section** — blue light concern for bedtime video use

### 8. WORD COUNT TARGET

Median ~2,000 words for top-10 guides. For asmrregistry.com: **1,500–2,000 words** editorial + structured filterable video section with 20–30 tagged entries. Minimum to compete: **1,400 words**.
