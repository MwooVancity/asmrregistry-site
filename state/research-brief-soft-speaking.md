Three groups: discovery (what is this trigger), comparison (vs whispering), video-finding (curated list). Dominant: navigational/informational — explain trigger + show best examples.

Target: "Soft speaking ASMR is a trigger in which the creator speaks at low volume using their full voice — not a breathy whisper — to produce a calm, intimate sound. Unlike whispering, soft speaking maintains vocal clarity and warmth, which makes it easier to follow for extended sessions."
