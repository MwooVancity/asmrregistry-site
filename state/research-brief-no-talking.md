# Content Brief: No Talking ASMR

## 1. SEARCH INTENT
Three buckets: sleep aid (dominant — want long-form 1-3hr ambient, no vocal distraction), study/focus (background ASMR without language processing competition), anxiety/sensory relief (tingles without social intimacy of whisper persona). Unifying intent: find videos or curated list of no-talking ASMR without sifting through talking/whispering channels.
