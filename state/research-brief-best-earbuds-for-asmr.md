## 5. CONTENT GAPS (missing from all top results)
1. Sleep use vs. awake listening need different picks (sleep = flush-fit, low-profile; awake = can handle over-ear fit)
2. Volume advice: ASMR tingles diminish at high volume; 30-40% is the sweet spot
3. ANC paradox: ANC noise floor is audible at quiet ASMR volumes — passive isolation via foam tips is cleaner
4. Codec matters for wireless: SBC degrades micro-detail; need aptX/AAC/LDAC
5. Eartip material: silicone causes ear fatigue in 60-90 min; foam tips (Comply) reduce fatigue + improve isolation
6. No result leads with a decision framework by use case

## 6. KEY BUYING FACTORS
- Driver type: dynamic drivers preferred; planar for critical listeners
- Soundstage/imaging: binaural ASMR needs 3D positional cues
- Frequency response: neutral/warm; avoid V-shaped (bass-boosted); whispers live at 2-8 kHz
- Passive isolation: good seal via foam tips; deep-fit IEMs over shallow buds
- Comfort for long sessions: low-profile housing, lightweight, flush design
- ANC noise floor: ANC often introduces hiss audible at ASMR volumes — disable or avoid
- Bluetooth codec (wireless): aptX/AAC/LDAC; avoid SBC-only
