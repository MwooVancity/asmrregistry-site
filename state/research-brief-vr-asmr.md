## VR ASMR — Content Brief for asmrregistry.com

### 1. SEARCH INTENT

Split intent between two audiences:

- **Consumers** (majority): already watch 2D ASMR and want to know if VR makes it better, which headset to use, and where to find the best videos. Core question: *"Is VR ASMR worth it and how do I watch it?"*
- **Curious newcomers**: discovered VR first (Meta Quest owners) looking for relaxation/sleep content in headset. Core question: *"What ASMR apps or videos work on my Quest?"*

Secondary: small but active niche of **VRChat ASMR creators**.

### 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY

**Paragraph snippet is winnable.** "What is VR ASMR" has a clear definitional gap. A tight 40-60 word paragraph defining both, naming the mechanism (spatial/binaural audio + 360° visuals + head-tracked sound), and naming the benefit (more immersive tingles than 2D) will win the snippet.

**List snippet secondary opportunity** for "how to watch VR ASMR without a headset."

1. What is VR ASMR and how is it different from regular ASMR?

Top 10 dominated by:
- YouTube channels and playlists (3-4 spots)
- Guide/explainer articles (3 spots): goforvr.com (~1,800-2,000w), inasmr.com (thin ~100w)
- App store pages (1-2 spots): Meta Horizon Store

**Missing format:** a curated video directory with editorial context.

### 6. RECOMMENDED SECTIONS

1. **What Makes VR ASMR Different** — spatial audio head-tracking vs. standard binaural, presence effect, why tingles intensify in 360°. Snippet-target.
2. **How to Watch: Headset vs. No Headset** — numbered list: Quest/PSVR2/PC VR, mobile cardboard, desktop browser (click-drag 360°)
3. **180° vs. 360° — Which Should You Watch** — 180° has higher resolution and closer-face presence; 360° wraps you in an environment
4. **Best VR ASMR Videos (Curated)** — directory payload. Tag by trigger type, format (180°/360°), and platform
5. **VR ASMR Apps for Meta Quest** — ASMRVR ($), ASMR Playground (Mixed Reality), YouTube VR (free)
6. **VRChat ASMR — The Live Experience** — how streamers do ASMR inside VRChat using binaural avatar systems
7. **Does VR ASMR Actually Work?** — cite the 2026 Wiley study + Monash asmVR multimodal research

### 7. CONTENT GAPS

- **No comparison of 180° vs. 360°** — creators and consumers both search this
- **No Meta Quest app roundup** — ASMRVR and ASMR Playground both exist but no editorial page covers both
- **No VRChat ASMR coverage** — active creator niche completely ignored by SEO content
- **No research citations** — 2026 Wiley comparative study and 2023 Monash asmVR paper unclaimed
- **No "watch without a headset" how-to** — goforvr.com mentions it but nobody owns the clean list
- **No trigger-tagged video index**
- **No binaural audio explainer in context of VR**

### 8. WORD COUNT TARGET

**Target: 1,400-1,800 words of editorial prose** plus the video directory cards. Compete on precision and unique angles (VRChat, 180°/360° distinction, Quest app roundup, research citation) not raw word count.
