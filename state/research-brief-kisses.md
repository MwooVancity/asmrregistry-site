## KISSES ASMR — Content Research Brief

### 1. SEARCH INTENT

Dual-stream intent — both converge on discovery and consumption, not information:

- **Primary:** Find specific kissing ASMR videos to watch right now — overwhelmingly for sleep onset. Searchers want a curated list/player of kiss-trigger videos, not an explainer article.
- **Secondary:** Validate the trigger — curious new ASMR listeners who've heard about kissing sounds but aren't sure if it's their thing.

The dominant use case is **sleep prep** (searches fire late at night, titles confirm: "kissing you to sleep," "goodnight kisses"). Secondary use is **relaxation/stress relief**.

### 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY

- "What is kissing ASMR" → paragraph snippet opportunity: 40–60 words defining the trigger, its effect (tingling sensation), and primary use case (sleep/relaxation).
- "Types of kissing ASMR" → ordered list snippet: 6–9 kiss types.

**Best bet:** Lead with crisp 50-word definition paragraph, then typed list of kiss variants.
