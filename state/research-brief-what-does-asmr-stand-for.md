## CONTENT BRIEF: "What Does ASMR Stand For"

### 1. SEARCH INTENT
Definitional/informational — first-time searchers who encountered the acronym and want plain-English explanation. They want: (1) what the letters spell out, (2) what that means, (3) whether it's real. Secondary: validation seekers. Tertiary: parents, journalists, skeptics.

### 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY
Paragraph snippet wins. Lead with one-sentence definition in first `<p>`, use exact phrase "ASMR stands for". Definition table (breaking each word) has realistic shot at table snippet.
