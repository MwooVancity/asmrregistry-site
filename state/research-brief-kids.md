## ASMR for Kids — Content Brief

### 1. SEARCH INTENT

Dual-audience keyword — two distinct searchers:

- **Parents** (primary): "Is this safe for my child? Will it help them sleep / calm down? What should I let them watch?" Informational + gate-keeping intent.
- **Kids age 8–14** (secondary): "Find me ASMR videos made for kids" — navigational/video-finding intent.

The page needs to serve both without alienating either. Parents want reassurance + curation. Kids want video discovery.

### 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY

**Paragraph snippet** currently dominates. A **"Is ASMR safe for kids?" definition block** with a clear yes/no lede, then a brief qualifier sentence, would be snippet-worthy. Competing pages bury the direct answer.

**Secondary opportunity:** A **bulleted list snippet** for "best ASMR triggers for kids" or "child-friendly ASMR types."

### 3. PEOPLE ALSO ASK

1. Is ASMR safe for kids?
2. What age can kids watch ASMR?
3. Can ASMR help kids sleep?
4. Is ASMR good for kids with ADHD or autism?
5. What are the best ASMR channels for kids?
6. Can ASMR be inappropriate — how do parents screen it?
7. Does ASMR actually work for children's anxiety?
8. What is the difference between ASMR and white noise for kids?

### 5. SERP CONTENT TYPE

Top 10 is split:

- **YouTube videos / playlists** (~4 results): WhispersRed Sleepy Children, dedicated kids ASMR playlists
- **Parenting editorial** (~3 results): Today's Parent, Metro Parent, iMOM — long-form "explainer + safety" articles, 800–1,400 words
- **Safety/parenting blogs** (~2 results): Safes.so, TherapyDial — listicle format
- **asmrregistry.com** (already ranking): directory/hub format

The directory format is the only one curating actual videos in context — structural advantage.

### 6. RECOMMENDED SECTIONS

1. **What is ASMR for kids** — plain-English definition, 3–4 sentences, optimized for paragraph snippet
2. **Is ASMR safe for kids?** — direct yes + 3 caveats (content screening, volume control, sleep crutch risk)
3. **Top child-friendly ASMR triggers** — curated list: whispered bedtime stories, nature sounds, slime/kinetic sand, soft drawing sounds, rain
4. **ASMR for kids with ADHD and autism** — sensory regulation angle
5. **ASMR for kids' sleep** — bedtime routine integration, 20–30 min cap guidance
6. **How to find safe ASMR content for kids** — YouTube Kids platform tip, pre-screen advice, red flags to avoid
7. **Curated video directory** — structured by use case: sleep / anxiety / focus / sensory play

### 7. CONTENT GAPS

- **No one curates actual safe videos with context.** Every editorial article says "check YouTube Kids" and stops there.
- **Age-specific guidance is missing.** "Kids" = 3-year-olds and 13-year-olds to most articles.
- **Focus/homework use case is ignored.** ADHD + ASMR for focus while doing homework — zero editorial coverage.
- **School anxiety trigger type** completely unaddressed.
- **The "my kid is INTO making ASMR" angle** — kids as creators, not just consumers.
- **Sensory processing disorder distinction** is absent.

### 8. WORD COUNT TARGET

**Target: 600–800 words of editorial prose** + the video directory listings. Do not inflate to 1,400 words — the directory content is the structural moat.
