## RESEARCH BRIEF: ASMR Eating Sounds
**For:** `asmrregistry.com/eating-sounds/`
**Date:** 2026-07-25

---

### 1. SEARCH INTENT

Searchers want two things, and the split matters:

**Primary (70%):** Find video content. They want a curated list of ASMR eating-sound videos to put on right now, for sleep, study, or anxiety relief. They are not researching the topic, they want to press play.

**Secondary (30%):** Understand the phenomenon. Why do eating sounds relax some people? Is it normal? What's the difference between ASMR eating and mukbang? This skews toward first-timers who discovered the trigger accidentally.

The page must serve both: video directory first, explanation second. Don't bury the directory under 1,200 words of prose.

---

### 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY

**Format that wins: paragraph snippet.**

The query "why do asmr eating sounds feel relaxing" pulls a paragraph-style snippet. The winning format is 40-60 words explaining the mechanism in plain language (rhythmic repetition, predictability, safety associations from childhood). No list, no table wins this query.

**List snippet opportunity:** "best foods for asmr eating sounds" can win a bulleted list (6-8 food types). This is a secondary snippet worth targeting in a dedicated section.

**Table snippet:** Low opportunity. No table format currently dominates for this query cluster.
