# Live ASMR — Research Brief

## 1. SEARCH INTENT
Primary (70%): Finder — "where can I watch ASMR right now, live?" Navigation/discovery, not learn.
Secondary (30%): Creator — "how do I stream ASMR live?" Setup tips, platform comparison.
Serve finder intent first.

## 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY
**Paragraph snippet** — no clean owner of "what is live ASMR" definitional paragraph. 40–60 word paragraph explaining real-time streaming + interactive chat trigger requests.
**List snippet** — "platforms where you can watch live ASMR" (Twitch, TikTok Live, YouTube Live, Kick, Instagram Live).

## 6. RECOMMENDED SECTIONS
1. What is live ASMR — definitional paragraph (snippet target)
2. Where to watch right now — platform breakdown: Twitch, TikTok Live, YouTube Live, Kick, Instagram Live
3. Live vs. pre-recorded — no post-production, real-time trigger requests, community feel
4. Most popular triggers in live streams — assortments, whispering, tapping, mouth sounds, roleplay

6. How to interact during a live stream — chatting, requesting triggers, tipping, raids

- No trigger-specific live stream finder

- No viewer etiquette guide

## 8. WORD COUNT TARGET
Target 1,000–1,400 words. Directory structure + cross-platform comparison is the real moat. Don't pad to 2,500+.
