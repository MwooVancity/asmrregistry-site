## RESEARCH BRIEF: ASMR Rain Sounds
**For:** `asmrregistry.com/rain/`
**Date:** 2026-07-25

---

### 1. SEARCH INTENT

Searchers are split across three primary intents:

- **Immediate access** (navigational/transactional): They want to press play on rain audio right now — for sleep, focus, or anxiety relief. These users bounce from Google directly to YouTube, Spotify, or Rainy Mood.
- **Discovery** (informational): They know rain sounds help them and want to find the best channels, videos, or apps they haven't tried yet. This is the directory's core user.
- **Validation** (informational): They want to understand why rain sounds feel calming — the science behind it — often searched alongside the audio.

The directory page should serve the discovery intent first: curated channels and videos they can't easily find by scrolling YouTube.

---

### 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY

**Format that wins:** Paragraph snippet (definition-style).

Google currently surfaces paragraph snippets answering "what is rain ASMR" and "why are rain sounds relaxing." The winning format is a 2-3 sentence paragraph explaining the mechanism (pink noise, amygdala dampening, nostalgia/safety association) followed by a use-case list. No table snippet opportunity exists for this keyword cluster. A list snippet ("best rain ASMR channels") is plausible but requires a clearly structured H2 + bullet list.

**Recommended snippet target:** Write a crisp 40-60 word definitional paragraph under the H1 answering "What is rain sounds ASMR?" — that is the paragraph Google wants to pull.
