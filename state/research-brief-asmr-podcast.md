# ASMR Podcast — Research Brief

## 1. SEARCH INTENT
Primary (70%): Discovery — "find ASMR content to listen to without a screen" (driving, lights off). Existing ASMR consumers wanting format upgrade.
Secondary (20%): Newcomer — "what is an ASMR podcast vs YouTube ASMR?"
Tertiary (10%): Creator/producer intent.
Dominant: navigational-to-discovery — find a specific show filtered by trigger type or use case.

## 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY
**Paragraph snippet** for "what is an ASMR podcast" — audio-specific definition gap.
Target: "An ASMR podcast is an audio-only recording of ASMR triggers — whispers, tapping, rain, soft speech — designed to be listened to without a screen. Unlike video ASMR, podcasts eliminate blue light disruption, making them better suited for falling asleep."
**List snippet** for "best ASMR podcasts" — competitive (Feedspot, Goodpods, Podchaser). Own the definition + trigger-filtered directory instead.

## 5. SERP CONTENT TYPE
Ranked list articles (Feedspot, Goodpods, Podchaser — generic, no trigger filtering) + platform directories + editorial explainers (vidpros.com ~2,200 words). Gap: zero pages filter by trigger type.

## 6. RECOMMENDED SECTIONS
1. What makes an ASMR podcast different from ASMR video — own the snippet
2. Which triggers work best in podcast format — whisper, soft speech, rain/nature, tapping survive; personal attention/visual roleplay lose effectiveness
3. ASMR podcasts by use case — sleep, focus/studying, anxiety, background
4. Where to find ASMR podcasts — Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube audio mode, dedicated sites
5. Featured ASMR podcast directory — trigger type, episode length, platform, use case
6. Audio vs. video ASMR — which is right for you — decision comparison
7. How episode length affects ASMR effectiveness — 15–30 min focus; 2–10 hours sleep

## 7. CONTENT GAPS
- No trigger-filtered navigation (group by "if your trigger is whisper, listen to X")
- No platform availability at a glance
- No episode length guidance (why 600-min episodes exist, when to choose short vs. long)
- No audio trigger science explanation
- No content for ASMR listeners who don't get tingles
- No freshness indicator (many directories list dead shows)

## 8. WORD COUNT TARGET
Minimum 1,200–1,500 words editorial prose + directory of 20–50 shows. Don't chase 3,000+.
