## SOAP CARVING ASMR — Content Brief

### 1. SEARCH INTENT

Searchers fall into two groups. **Passive viewers** (majority) want to find videos to watch for relaxation, sleep aid, or stress relief. **Curious newcomers** want to understand why soap carving sounds are satisfying before committing to watching. No transactional intent (nobody is buying soap to carve). The trigger appeal is **audio-first** — the crunching, crackling, scraping sounds are the draw, not the visual art.

### 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY

**Paragraph snippet** is the likely winner. A tightly written definition explaining the trigger (crunchy soap sounds → ASMR tingles → relaxation/sleep) would compete for the snippet. A **list snippet** is secondary opportunity for "best soap carving ASMR channels" or "types of soap sounds in ASMR."
