## Sand ASMR — Content Brief

### 1. SEARCH INTENT

Searchers split into two groups:

- **Passive consumers** (majority): want to find videos to watch right now for sleep, anxiety relief, or general relaxation. Want a curated list of the best creators and videos.
- **Active discoverers**: just found the trigger via TikTok (Sand.Tagious has ~19M TikTok followers) and want to go deeper.

Intent is **transactional-to-navigational**: "give me the best sand ASMR to watch." Page must lead with video access, not explanation.

### 2. FEATURED SNIPPET OPPORTUNITY

**Paragraph snippet** is most likely winner. Nobody has a clean definitional paragraph covering sub-types (kinetic, beach, colored, moon sand) in one block. A 40–60 word paragraph defining sand ASMR + naming the 3 dominant sub-types is a real snippet grab.

For "kinetic sand ASMR" specifically, a **list snippet** (techniques: cutting, crushing, scooping, pouring) is available.

### 5. SERP CONTENT TYPE

Top 10 dominated by **YouTube video results**. Organic text results sparse and weak — mostly Nerdist, Inverse, and Spoon University, all 5+ years old. No authoritative hub page for sand ASMR creators. Gap for asmrregistry.com.

### 6. RECOMMENDED SECTIONS

1. **What is sand ASMR** — 60-word answer-first definition covering the trigger type, why it works
2. **Types of sand used** — kinetic vs. moon sand vs. colored sand vs. beach/nature sand; each has distinct sound profile and audience
3. **Best sand ASMR creators** — Sand.Tagious, Sand Cutting ASMR channel, Sand Soundtagious, Sand One
4. **Best sand ASMR videos for sleep** — curated list with use-case framing (no talking, long duration)
5. **Kinetic sand ASMR specifically** — its own section; covers cutting, scooping, crushing, poking techniques
6. **Sand ASMR on TikTok vs YouTube** — TikTok = short-form/visual satisfying, YouTube = long-form/sleep use
7. **How to experience sand ASMR at home** — links to make kinetic sand

### 7. CONTENT GAPS

- Creator comparison table (who posts where, what sub-type, subscriber count)
- Distinction between sand ASMR as tactile manipulation trigger vs. ambient nature sound (beach waves)
- **No talking / no music** filter — major viewer preference
- Duration guidance — sleep users need 1hr+ compilations
- Why the ASMR community debated "slicing sand" videos
- Accessibility angle — sand ASMR is one of the most beginner-friendly triggers

### 8. WORD COUNT TARGET

**900–1,200 words minimum.** Ceiling around 1,500. Aim for 1,000 words with tight section headers and a strong creator table.
